Tracks

Note: The ranking criteria for the individual tracks are described under rules.

Solvers can be submitted for evaluation in the following tracks.

Main Tracks for Exact Solvers

  • Unweighted: Combines the industrial and crafted unweighted and unweighted partial MaxSAT categories from previous MaxSAT evaluations. Purely randomly generated instances are not included.
  • Weighted: Combines the industrial and crafted weighted and weighted partial MaxSAT categories from previous MaxSAT evaluations. All benchmarks will be truly weighted, i.e., contain soft clauses with different weights. Purely randomly generated instances are not included.

Main Tracks for Anytime Solvers

  • Two tracks: unweighted and weighted (following the categorization of the main tracks), for anytime solvers are organized.
  • Every anytime solver participating in one or both of the tracks will be evaluated using

    three distinct per-instance time limits: 60 seconds, 300 seconds, and a unspecified timeout that will be drawn randomly from the range 900 to 1800s after solver submissions have closed. Note that, in contrast to previous years, this means that all solvers participatiung in the anytime track should be prepared to handle timeouts up to 1800 seconds. Results will be reported separately for all timeouts.
